### [HALCYON](https://github.com/veereshr4446/HALCYON) &nbsp;
![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Frontend_prototype-00D4FF?style=flat-square&logoColor=black)
![Commits](https://img.shields.io/badge/162_commits-555?style=flat-square)
[![Live](https://img.shields.io/badge/live_demo-000?style=flat-square&logo=vercel&logoColor=white)](https://halcyon-student-dashboard.vercel.app/)

A student dashboard for RYMEC that brings academics, productivity and student life into one interface. Attendance and CIE analytics, an SGPA calculator, an attendance what-if calculator, a Pomodoro focus timer with history, a planner and notes, a faculty directory, an editable profile, study groups, a leaderboard, and dark/light mode.

Built as a working frontend prototype for demonstration. It is not connected to any college system and does not collect any student data.

### [Site Audit Form](https://github.com/veereshr4446/site-audit-form)
![In use](https://img.shields.io/badge/In_active_use-3FB950?style=flat-square)
![Week 1](https://img.shields.io/badge/10_engineers_%C2%B7_70_submissions-555?style=flat-square)

A mobile-first form for field engineers at JEF Techno, Bangalore. It replaces manual spreadsheet entry with direct on-site submission of site details, team and schedule, status and uploads, and location.

Submissions go to a private Google Sheet through Google Apps Script. The Sheet is both the live database and the admin dashboard, with a custom menu for Excel export and cleanup. The public form cannot read other submissions.

> The team estimated the old workflow at **2 to 3 hours per site**. The form takes about **2 minutes**.

Built independently, from requirements to deployment and support.

### [CROPIX](https://github.com/veereshr4446/CROPIX)
![MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-97CA00?style=flat-square)
![Client-side](https://img.shields.io/badge/100%25_client--side-00D4FF?style=flat-square&logoColor=black)

A fast browser-based image cropper built for sprite sheets and multi-region images. Upload or paste an image, select several regions, then move, resize, zoom, pan, undo and redo. A live gallery previews every crop, and one click exports them all as separate PNGs in a ZIP.

Images never leave the browser. No backend, no login, no data collection. Works with mouse, touch, drag and drop, and keyboard shortcuts.

### [Crop-Zenith](https://github.com/veereshr4446/Crop-Zenith)
![MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-97CA00?style=flat-square)
![Crops](https://img.shields.io/badge/6_crops-3FB950?style=flat-square)

A crop yield prediction app that helps farmers and agronomists make data-driven decisions. Enter temperature, rainfall, soil pH and NPK levels for rice, wheat, maize, soybean, cotton or sugarcane. It returns predicted yield, total production, a confidence score, a soil health index, and tailored recommendations.

A weighted scoring algorithm drives the estimate, with interactive bar and radar charts.
